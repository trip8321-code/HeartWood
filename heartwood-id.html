    Identify from a photo

    How to take a good ID photo
    1. Leaf: the whole leaf and twig on a plain background, both sides, showing whether leaves attach opposite or alternate.
    2. Bark: one shot from about 2 ft back and one close up, on dry bark.
    3. End grain: plane or razor the cut clean, use flat daylight, and shoot close with a 10x loupe if you have one.
    4. Board face: show a plain-sawn and a quartersawn face if available, dry and unfinished.
    5. Always: daylight, no flash, a coin or ruler in frame, two or three shots, and note the smell and weight.
